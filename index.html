When the AI books the table for them

Direct answer: Agentic AI means an assistant that does not just answer “where should we eat” but goes on to check hours, compare menus, and call or book on the person’s behalf. Agents can only act on what they can read: structured hours and menus, a real phone number in a tel link, a reservation link with a plain label, and consistent facts across the web. A restaurant that publishes those gets picked; one with a PDF menu and a contact form image does not exist to the agent.

What an agent needs from your site

  • OpeningHoursSpecification, including holiday hours, so it never sends someone to a locked door
  • Menu, MenuSection and MenuItem schema with prices and dietary flags
  • acceptsReservations and a labeled reservation or order link it can follow
  • A tel: link — not a phone number pasted into an image
  • Address, geo coordinates and parking notes in plain text
  • llms.txt and robots rules that allow GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended
  • One paragraph about the room: noise, kids, patio, accessibility

We build every one of those into every restaurant site, and we test it by asking the assistants ourselves each month: who do they name, and can they act on it?

The 5-minute self-check

Do these on your phone right now. Every unchecked box is a table going somewhere else.

  • Search your cuisine + “near me” from the parking lot. Are you in the 3-pack?
  • Ask Siri and Google Assistant the same question. Do they say your name?
  • Ask ChatGPT or Gemini “where should I eat [your cuisine] in [your city]?”
  • Time your website on cellular. Over 2 seconds is losing people.
  • Is your menu a PDF? Then Google and AI can’t read a single dish.
  • Do your hours match on Google, Apple Maps, Yelp and DoorDash?
  • When was your last Google photo posted? Your last review reply?
  • Does your site have Restaurant schema? (View source, search “schema.org”.)
  • Is there one tap-to-call, one tap-for-directions, one tap-to-order on the first screen?
  • Can a first-time visitor tell what you serve, where you are and when you’re open in five seconds?
