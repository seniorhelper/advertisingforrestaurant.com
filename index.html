AI answers and voice search are the new page one

Direct answer: AI assistants and voice assistants don’t show ten blue links — they say one to three names. They pick from Google Business Profile, structured data, review text and plain-language pages. AIO/GEO for a restaurant means making those sources complete, consistent and quotable.

🗣️ “Hey Google, what’s a good brunch spot with a patio near me?”

The assistant reads your Business Profile attributes (outdoor seating, brunch hours), your reviews (“great patio”), and a page on your site that literally says “brunch on the patio, Saturday and Sunday 9–2.” We make all three say the same thing.

🤖 “ChatGPT, where should I take my parents for dinner in Denver?”

The model cites pages it can parse: menu with prices, a plain paragraph about the room and the noise level, reservation details, and reviews it can summarize. A PDF menu is invisible; a Menu-schema page is a citation.

🔎 Google AI Overview: “best sushi open late”

Overviews pull from pages with clear headings that match the question, hours in OpeningHoursSpecification, and consistent facts across the web. Our location pages are written as questions and answers on purpose.

📱 Apple Maps & Siri

Apple Business Connect is a separate listing most restaurants never claim. We claim it, match it to Google, and add the same photos and hours so Siri users get the same answer.

The 5-minute self-check

Do these on your phone right now. Every unchecked box is a table going somewhere else.

  • Search your cuisine + “near me” from the parking lot. Are you in the 3-pack?
  • Ask Siri and Google Assistant the same question. Do they say your name?
  • Ask ChatGPT or Gemini “where should I eat [your cuisine] in [your city]?”
  • Time your website on cellular. Over 2 seconds is losing people.
  • Is your menu a PDF? Then Google and AI can’t read a single dish.
  • Do your hours match on Google, Apple Maps, Yelp and DoorDash?
  • When was your last Google photo posted? Your last review reply?
  • Does your site have Restaurant schema? (View source, search “schema.org”.)
  • Is there one tap-to-call, one tap-for-directions, one tap-to-order on the first screen?
  • Can a first-time visitor tell what you serve, where you are and when you’re open in five seconds?
