Questions from the counter

What is advertising for a restaurant, really?

For a restaurant, advertising is every way a hungry person finds you before they pick somewhere else: the Google Maps pack, the first page of Google, the answer an AI assistant gives, a paid ad at 5:45 pm, and a website that loads instantly and shows the menu. We run all of those as one system.

How long does restaurant SEO take to work?

Google Business Profile and Maps improvements usually show within weeks. Organic rankings for competitive searches like “best sushi near me” typically build over 3 to 6 months as content, reviews, citations and technical fixes compound.

Why a custom-coded website instead of WordPress?

No plugins to break, no theme bloat, no monthly security patches, and pages that load in under a second on a phone. Speed and clean structured data are ranking signals, and a hand-coded site gives us full control of both.

What does it cost?

Custom-coded restaurant websites lease from $99 per month with a buyout option at 24 to 36 months. SEO, AI search optimization and ad management are quoted per market. Call 1-800-481-8638 for a number.

Do you work with restaurants outside Colorado?

Yes. We are based in Denver and work with restaurants across the United States. Local SEO is local everywhere; the playbook travels.

What is AIO or GEO for restaurants?

AIO (AI optimization) and GEO (generative engine optimization) mean structuring your restaurant’s facts, menu, hours and reviews so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI Overviews cite you when someone asks where to eat. It is the new front page.
